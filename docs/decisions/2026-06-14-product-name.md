## Finding

The English single-word AI/voice/CS namespace is **exhaustively squatted**. A clean, strong,
ownable single-word `.com` does not exist here. Brute-force hunting cannot succeed without
changing one of the constraints below.

## Decision — DEFERRED pending founder's constraint choice

The reachable namespace depends on which constraint we relax. Options (a spend/strategy fork):
1. **Accept `.ai`/`.io` TLD** — widens single-word options; normal for AI products.
2. **Budget a premium `.com` purchase** ($3k–$30k) — target buyable one-word domains.
3. **Two-word / compound brand** on a free `.com` (e.g. distinctive compound or "Get-/Use-").
4. **Pure abstract coinage** (Twilio/Plaid/Brex-style invented word) — iterate until clear.

Once chosen, run a focused verified sprint to land 2–3 candidates clean on the agreed
constraint, then: WHOIS + USPTO/EUIPO (Classes 9/38/42) + live-company check before locking;
only then rename the codebase ("Bridge"/"LinguaBridge") in one pass.
